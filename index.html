01 / ABOUT

LESS THEORY.
MORE BUILDING.

Mumukshu Labs is a student-driven technology community affiliated with Hack Club. Mumukshu Labs is built around curiosity, creativity and collaboration.

We learn by doing, building projects, experimenting with technology and helping each other turn ideas into reality.

02 / WHAT WE DO

WE ROCK TOGETHER
AT EVERYTHING WE DO IN TECH.

  • Build projects → software, hardware, and creative technology projects.
  • Learn together → share skills, experiment, and learn beyond the classroom.
  • Explore technology → try new tools, ideas, and areas of tech.
  • Organize workshops and events → create opportunities for students to learn, participate, and experience technology.
  • Collaborate → work with students, clubs, and communities.
  • Turn ideas into reality → learn by actually making things.
