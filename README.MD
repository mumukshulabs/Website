# Mumukshu Labs — Website

The official website of **Mumukshu Labs**, a student-led technology community based in Nepal.

🌐 **Website:** https://labs.mumukshu.me/

## About Mumukshu Labs

Mumukshu Labs brings together students who share an interest in technology and want to explore what they can create with it.

We aim to make technology more accessible to students by creating opportunities to explore programming, software development, game development, and other areas through hands-on projects, workshops, collaborative activities, and community events.

We believe that you don't need to be an expert to get started. Curiosity, creativity, and the willingness to experiment are enough.

## About This Repository

This repository contains the source code for the official Mumukshu Labs website.

It serves as our public-facing platform to introduce the organization, share information about our activities, and help interested students discover and connect with our community.

## Our Mission

To bring curious minds together, encourage experimentation, and create opportunities for students to explore technology and turn their ideas into something meaningful.
